Techfest 2026

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, commonly known as IIT Bombay. The festival includes robotics competitions, hackathons, AI and coding workshops, tech exhibitions, guest lectures by scientists and entrepreneurs, gaming events, and live techno entertainment.
